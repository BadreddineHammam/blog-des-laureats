# Blog des Lauréats — Project Plan

## 1. Project Presentation

Blog des Lauréats is a platform for sharing the success stories of former students.

It has two parts:

- **Website:** Administrators publish and manage articles. Visitors read the stories.
- **Mobile app:** Students and visitors read articles on a smartphone.

**Example:** A former student finds a good job after training. They write an article about their journey. Other students read it for inspiration and advice.

The platform does not start development in this document. This document explains the project scope, goals, people, and risks.

## 2. SMART Goals

| Goal | What success looks like | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Publish useful stories | At least 12 stories are approved and published | Before launch |
| Help users find articles | Users find articles by title, category, or keyword | Before testing |
| Help administrators manage articles | An administrator creates, edits, publishes, or archives an article in less than 10 minutes | Before delivery |
| Support mobile reading | The app works on 3 common smartphone sizes | Before mobile validation |
| Keep content accurate | 100% of published articles are reviewed first | Continuous |
| Protect data | Unauthorized users cannot access administrator functions | Before production |

**Main purpose:** Motivate students, preserve useful experiences, improve the center's reputation, and help learners make career choices.

## 3. Project Scope

| Part | In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|---|
| Website | Article pages, search, categories, and responsive design | Separate social network or desktop app |
| Mobile app | Reading, navigation, and search | Publishing articles from the app |
| Administrators | Create, edit, preview, publish, and archive articles | Server and infrastructure management |
| Content | Student profiles, success stories, and approved images | Illegal, false, or advertising content |
| User experience | Simple navigation and clear reading pages | AI recommendations or advanced personalization |
| Comments | Only if the Product Owner approves | Forums and public messaging |
| Security | Administrator login, access roles, and backups | Security certification or separate penetration testing |

**Scope rule:** Only approved features needed to publish and read success stories are included.

## 4. People Involved

| Person or group | What they do |
|---|---|
| Product Owner — Group 1 | Approves requirements and priorities |
| Scrum Master / Project Manager | Plans work and removes blockers |
| Development Team | Builds, tests, and documents the platform |
| Junior Developers | Complete assigned tasks and ask for help early |
| Administrators | Create, review, publish, and archive articles |
| Former Students | Provide accurate success stories |
| Current Students and Visitors | Read articles and give feedback |
| Center Management | Approves the project and provides support |
| Teachers and Coaches | Help review content |
| Technical Support | Helps protect accounts and availability |

## 5. Risks and Prevention

| Risk | How likely | Impact | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Too many features | Medium | High | Use the scope table and ask the Product Owner before changes |
| Articles contain wrong information | Medium | High | A teacher or editor reviews every article |
| Students provide few stories | Medium | Medium | Use a simple submission form, an example, and reminders |
| Web and mobile designs differ | Medium | Medium | Use the same design rules and agreed screens |
| Administrator account is hacked | Low | High | Use strong passwords, access roles, HTTPS, and backups |
| Private information is published | Medium | High | Ask permission and remove sensitive information |
| Development becomes complex | Medium | High | Divide work into small tasks and test often |
| App is slow or unavailable | Low | Medium | Compress images and use caching and monitoring |
| App does not work on older phones | Medium | Medium | Test common screen sizes and keep the design light |
| Team member becomes unavailable | Low | High | Share documentation and assign backup owners |
| Requirements change | High | Medium | Record changes and get Product Owner approval |
| Image permission is missing | Medium | Medium | Use licensed images and keep written permission |

Review major risks every week. Assign one person to follow each risk and update prevention plans when requirements change.
